# Промты: оружие башни и слизень

Сейчас эти картинки нарисованы кодом (`tools/draw_tower_items.py`) как
временные. Когда сгенерируешь настоящие — режем в `assets/items/` под теми же
именами, игра подхватит сама.

Общий стиль (добавлять к каждому): cartoon game item, thick dark brown
outline (#1a1410), soft cel shading, one small white highlight, flat colors,
centered, transparent background, no text, no shadow on ground, same style
as a white cartoon glove holding grenade, 512×512.

## 1. Гиря — `hand_weight.png` и `shot_weight.png`

- **shot:** a heavy black cast-iron kettlebell, round body with a thick arched
  handle on top, small yellow label plate on the front, slightly worn metal,
  cartoon game item.
- **hand:** the same kettlebell held by a white cartoon glove from the
  bottom-left, the glove grips the handle.

## 2. Липучка — `hand_sticky.png` и `shot_sticky.png`

- **shot:** a round bomb made of bright lime-green slime, glossy, three short
  slime drips hanging from the bottom, a short black fuse on top with an
  orange spark, cartoon game item.
- **hand:** the same slime bomb held by a white cartoon glove, a string of
  slime stretching between the glove and the bomb.

## 3. Батут — `hand_spring.png` и `shot_spring.png`

- **shot:** a small jump pad: dark grey metal base, a big silver coil spring,
  a round red rubber top pad with a light stripe, side view, cartoon game item.
- **hand:** the same jump pad carried by a white cartoon glove under the
  base.

## 4. Слизень — `mon_slime.png`

A cute but angry green slime monster, teardrop blob shape, glossy lime
body, two big white eyes with black pupils and angry eyebrows, small
highlight on top, sitting on the ground, side view, cartoon game sprite,
same outline style.

Кактус и Великан — эмодзи 🌵 и 👹 из набора Twemoji, рисовать не нужно.
